ABOUT CODEUTSAVA

🛸

THE MISSION

CodeUtsava is the flagship annual hackathon of the Turing Club of Programmers (TCP), NIT Raipur. Now entering its 10th orbit around innovation, we are Central India's largest and most celebrated coding event.

🌌

THE UNIVERSE

Hosted at NIT Raipur — a hub of technological excellence — CodeUtsava 10.0 brings together brilliant minds from across the country to solve real-world problems through code.

⚡

THE FORCE

From AI/ML to Web Development, Cybersecurity to IoT, our diverse tracks ensure every coder finds their frontier. Workshops, mentorship, and networking with industry leaders await.

// MISSION RULES

ASTRONAUT GUIDELINES

01
👥

CREW SIZE

Teams can have 2 to 4 members. Solo applications will be considered as teams of 1. Mix of skills strongly recommended.

02
🎓

ELIGIBILITY

Open to all undergraduate and postgraduate students currently enrolled in any college/university in India. Valid ID required.

03
💡

ORIGINAL WORK

All code must be written during the hackathon. Using open-source libraries is allowed. Pre-built projects are strictly prohibited.

04
📱

SUBMISSION

Submit your project via the official platform before the deadline. Include a demo video, GitHub repo link, and documentation.

05
🧑‍⚖️

JUDGING

Projects are judged on Innovation, Technical Complexity, Design & UX, Impact, and Presentation quality.

06
🤝

CODE OF CONDUCT

All participants must adhere to the code of conduct. Respectful behavior, inclusivity, and fair play are mandatory.
